Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains how the Hisham Automation application ("the App") handles information when it accesses Google user data.

1. Who uses the App

The App is a private automation tool used only by its owner and by business clients who explicitly connect their own Google account to it. It is not offered to the general public.

2. Data we access

  • Google Drive: reading specific files the account owner selects (for example, a knowledge-base document) so an AI assistant can answer customer questions.
  • Google Sheets: reading and writing rows in spreadsheets the account owner selects (for example, chat logs and conversation status).

3. How we use the data

Data is used only to run the automations the account owner has configured. We do not sell, rent, or share Google user data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted on a private n8n server. Data stays in the owner's own Google Drive and Sheets and is processed only when an automation runs.

5. Limited Use

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Revoking access

You can remove the App's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page. You can also ask us to delete any related data by email.
