## The bar (what wins)
- **A tactile, physical world with material**: threads, paper, wood, puppets, ink. Shadows, texture, light.
- **One continuous thing draws the next shot** (thread, pen line, receipt, lamp light). Nothing pops in from nowhere.
- **One space, one travelling camera**; and nothing from the previous shot half-visible at the frame edge.
- **A real ad message with conflict**: a pain, a dare, a guessing game, a match, an offer. The viewer must want to tap.
- **Constant motion**: camera push-ins, kicks on hits, sway; something new every 2–4 s.
- **Local flavour helps** (khayal el-zell, tarboosh, Eid, tea glass, Egyptian match commentary).

## Never again
- Flat UI: rectangles/cards/phone mockups/chips that only scale in and out → "HTML page".
- Spectacle without a selling message (roller coaster).
- Static camera / elements that merely appear ("dead").
- Garish arcade colours, harsh flashes, eye strain.
- Recycling an earlier ad's look, hook or palette: each of the five must look and open differently.

## Hooks
- Lands in the first 1.5–2 s; no filler before it ("يلا نشوف" was cut).
- On-screen hook beats run fast (5-second countdown in ~1.5 s).
- Best performers: dare to the viewer, guessing game, challenge ("جرّب تحرجه"), "ماتدفعش ولا جنيه… قبل ما تجرّب".

- Voice: Gemini TTS **Laomedeia** (young woman, mid-20s). Brisk but controlled ≈2.5 words/s ("she runs" if pushed).
  Give style instructions + inline tags ([short pause], [teasing] …) as copy-ready code blocks.

- Wording: "من بيانات تجارتك"; deal = **"عرض الإطلاق"** (not coupon); avoid the calendar wording, prefer data/database/sheet.
- Claims only from the site: setup 4,000 EGP; launch discount on the **monthly subscription** until **14 June 2027**, no %.

## Craft checklist (do every time, unasked)
1. Propose hook + look + shot table on the real VO timing; get approval before building.
2. Tighten VO gaps (or add breathing room) without changing words; say what you did.
3. Logo: `logo_name.jpg` crop **300, 540, 1790×690** (bubble glow never clipped); keep it close to its line.
4. Every element sits on its surface when it appears; check the frame just before each new line.
5. Icons readable at a glance (filled crescent + stars, clock with hour marks).
6. Last ~3 s: clean CTA + 010 9311 4446, nothing from the previous shot in frame.
7. Contact sheet → critique → fix → full render → **count frames** → pull frames from the encode → then send.
8. Deliver 9:16 1080×1920 60 fps −14 LUFS; send in chat (Drive upload from the cloud session is capped ~3 MB).
