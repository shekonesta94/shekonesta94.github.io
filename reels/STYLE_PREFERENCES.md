# Nesta reels — Hisham's standing preferences

Read this before writing any script or any frame. Every point here came from a revision he had to ask for.

## What made ads 1 and 2 land (the bar)
- A **tactile world with material**: threads with depth and shadow (ad 1), paper cut-outs on a sand table (ad 2). Not flat UI.
- **One continuous object draws the next shot**: the thread, the pen line. Nothing pops in from nowhere.
- **One room, one camera**: shots are places in the same space and the camera travels between them. No full-frame swap,
  and nothing from the previous shot left half-visible at the edge, then vanishing.
- A **story with a real conflict** tied to a business pain, not a "ride" or a showcase.

## Rejected — never again
- **"Looks like an HTML page"**: rectangles, cards, phone mockups and UI chips that just scale in and out (ad 3 v1, ad 4 train).
- **Spectacle with no ad message** (roller coaster): beautiful but nobody knows why to tap.
- **Dead execution**: static camera, elements that only appear. There must be motion between every line.
- Eye-straining looks: harsh flashes, garish arcade colours.

## Hooks
- Strong hook in the first 1.5–2 s; no filler words before it ("يلا نشوف" got cut).
- Fast hook visuals: a 5-second countdown runs in ~1.5 s on screen, not 3 s.
- Every hook different across the five ads; no repeated visual identity between ads.

## Script / voice
- Egyptian Arabic, short, light, punchy. One idea about every 5 s.
- Voice: Gemini TTS **Laomedeia**, brisk but controlled (~2.5 words/s), never rushed.
- Wording: "من بيانات تجارتك" (not "بياناتك إنت"); call the deal **"عرض الإطلاق"** (not coupon).
- Only claims that are on the site; launch discount is on the monthly subscription until 14 June 2027, no invented %.
- No calendar station wording he dislikes; prefer data/database/sheet.

## Craft
- Transition every 3–5 s, smooth and motivated.
- Last ~3 s: hold on WhatsApp CTA + number 010 9311 4446, nothing from the previous shot in frame.
- Logo: crop `logo_name.jpg` at **300, 540, 1790×690** so the bubble glow is never clipped. Keep the logo close to its line.
- Show the full plan (look, shot table on the VO timing) and get approval before building.
- Before sending: render the full film, **count frames**, pull frames from the encoded file.
- Durable deliverables: 9:16, 1080×1920, 60 fps, -14 LUFS.
